My story

Strategy to launch, without the gaps

I'm Abdul Rehman — a full-stack digital marketer from Pakistan. I connect search, paid media and technology into one plan, so nothing falls between the cracks.

I started where most marketers never go — inside the technology itself. Learning how websites are actually built changed the way I run campaigns: I don't just buy traffic, I shape the pages, tracking and follow-up systems that turn that traffic into revenue. When search, advertising and development are planned together, the customer journey becomes clearer — and the work becomes easier to measure.

Today I work across SEO, paid acquisition, websites and automation so the message, traffic source and conversion experience support each other. SEO services that compound over time, Google Ads and Meta Ads managed against real return on spend, WordPress and Shopify development, and AI automation that answers leads while you sleep.

Instead of adding activity for its own sake, I identify the most valuable constraint in your business, define the next practical move and build around outcomes you can actually track. Every engagement follows the same logic — discover the audience, demand and conversion barriers; build fast experiences and campaigns made for action; improve by measuring, testing and scaling what performs.

My rule is simple — every recommendation must be explainable in plain language and measurable in your reports. If it doesn't move leads, sales or efficiency, we don't do it.

My approach in practice

Audit first, promises later. I look at your website, ad accounts, analytics and competitors before recommending anything. The plan is ranked by what should bring revenue soonest — not by what's easiest to sell.

Tracking before traffic. Measurement is installed before a single rupee is spent, so every click, call and sale can be traced back to the decision that caused it.

Plain English, always. You'll never get a report full of vanity metrics or a strategy hidden behind jargon. If I can't explain it simply, I haven't understood it well enough.

How I work

Practical, transparent, data-led

Four principles guide every engagement — from a one-week audit to a year-long retainer. They're how I make sure the work stays honest, measurable and built to last.

🧭

Data-led decisions over guesswork

Every recommendation starts from your analytics, your market and your competitors — not trends, hunches or what worked for someone else. If the data doesn't support it, we don't do it. That discipline keeps budgets focused on moves with the highest probability of paying back.

🎯

Conversion-first user experiences

Traffic is only half the job. Pages, forms, product pages and checkout flows are designed around one question: what makes this visitor act? Speed, clarity and trust signals are engineered in — not added as an afterthought once the ads are already running.

💬

Clear reporting and communication

You always know what's happening, why it's happening and what it costs. Monthly reporting ties every activity back to leads, sales and spend — in plain English, with what changes next and why. No vanity metrics, no jargon walls, no surprises.

🧮

Scalable systems, not quick patches

Quick patches create tomorrow's problems. I build with clean structure — documented campaigns, organised code, automations you can understand — so your website, ad accounts and workflows keep working as you grow instead of collapsing under their own weight.
